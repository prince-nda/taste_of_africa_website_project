Jollof Rice

The smoky, tomato-red soul of every West African party.

Born from the Wolof Empire of Senegambia, jollof travelled across West Africa and became a source of friendly rivalry between Nigeria and Ghana. Long-grain rice simmers in a rich pepper and tomato base until each grain turns deep orange, with a prized smoky crust at the bottom of the pot.

1h 15m Serves 6 Nigeria

Ingredients

Servings 6
  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 plum tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • Bay leaves, thyme, curry powder
  • ⅓ cup vegetable oil

Nutrition (per serving)

Calories 420 kcal
Carbs 68 g
Protein 9 g
Fat 12 g
Cooking instructions +
  1. 01

    Blend tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnets and one onion until smooth.

  2. 02

    Fry the remaining sliced onion in oil, then add tomato paste and fry for 5 minutes.

  3. 03

    Pour in the blended base and cook down for 20 minutes until the oil rises.

  4. 04

    Add stock, spices and washed rice. Cover tightly with foil and the lid.

  5. 05

    Cook on low heat for 30 minutes, letting the bottom catch slightly for smoky flavour.
