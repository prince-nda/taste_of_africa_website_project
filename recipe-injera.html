Ethiopia · East Africa

Injera & Wot

A spongy sourdough flatbread that is plate, spoon and meal.

Made from teff , a tiny grain native to the Ethiopian highlands, injera ferments for days to gain its tangy flavour and signature bubbles. It is shared from one platter, topped with colourful wots — a practice called gursha celebrates feeding one another.

3 days (fermenting) Serves 6 Ethiopia

Ingredients

Servings 6
  • 2 cups teff flour
  • 3 cups water
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Berbere spice blend
  • Red lentils for misir wot
  • Niter kibbeh (spiced butter)
  • Red onions

Nutrition (per serving)

Calories 380 kcal
Carbs 70 g
Protein 14 g
Fiber 12 g
Cooking instructions +
  1. 01

    Mix teff flour and water; cover and ferment at room temperature for 2–3 days.

  2. 02

    Pour off any liquid on top, add salt and stir to a thin batter.

  3. 03

    Pour in a spiral onto a hot non-stick pan; cover once bubbles appear.

  4. 04

    Cook until the edges lift — never flip. Cool on a cloth.

  5. 05

    Serve with misir wot simmered in berbere and niter kibbeh.
