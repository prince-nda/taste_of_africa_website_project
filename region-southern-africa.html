Culinary region

Southern Africa

From Cape Town to Durban, Southern African food blends Indigenous, Dutch, Cape Malay and Indian traditions. Maize porridge, open-fire braai and fragrant curries bring families together around the same table.

Staples

  • Maize (pap)
  • Sorghum
  • Beans
  • Sweet potato
  • Bread

Cooking techniques

  • Braai (open-fire grilling)
  • Potjie (cast-iron pot stews)
  • Curry simmering
  • Biltong curing

Signature spices

  • Durban masala
  • Peri-peri
  • Curry leaf
  • Cape Malay masala
