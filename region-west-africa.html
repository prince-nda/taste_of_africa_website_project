Culinary region

West Africa

From Dakar to Lagos, West African kitchens are built on rice, yams, cassava and plantain. Stews are slow-simmered in rich tomato and pepper bases, and meals are shared from a common bowl.

Staples

  • Rice
  • Yam
  • Cassava
  • Plantain
  • Black-eyed peas

Cooking techniques

  • One-pot simmering
  • Pounding (fufu)
  • Open-fire grilling (suya)

Signature spices

  • Scotch bonnet
  • Grains of paradise
  • Dawadawa
  • Uda pepper
