Lamb Tagine

Slow-cooked lamb, apricots and almonds under a clay cone.

Named after the conical clay pot it is cooked in, the tagine is a Berber tradition refined in the imperial kitchens of Fez and Marrakech. Steam circulates inside the cone, keeping meat tender while sweet fruit and warm spices melt together.

2h 30m Serves 4 Morocco

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 1 kg lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 200 g dried apricots
  • 80 g toasted almonds
  • 2 onions, grated
  • Ras el hanout, cinnamon, ginger, saffron
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • Fresh coriander
  • Olive oil

Nutrition (per serving)

Calories 610 kcal
Carbs 38 g
Protein 42 g
Fat 32 g
Cooking instructions +
  1. 01

    Rub lamb with spices and marinate for at least 1 hour.

  2. 02

    Brown the lamb in olive oil inside the tagine base, then add onions.

  3. 03

    Add water to half-cover, close the lid and simmer gently for 1.5 hours.

  4. 04

    Add apricots and honey; cook 30 more minutes until glossy.

  5. 05

    Garnish with almonds and coriander. Serve with couscous or bread.
