Culinary region

North Africa

Stretching from Morocco to Egypt, North African food blends Berber, Arab, Andalusian and Ottoman influences. Couscous is the beloved staple and sweet-savoury combinations define the cuisine.

Staples

  • Couscous
  • Semolina
  • Chickpeas
  • Dates
  • Olives

Cooking techniques

  • Tagine slow-cooking
  • Steaming couscous
  • Preserving lemons

Signature spices

  • Ras el hanout
  • Saffron
  • Cumin
  • Harissa
