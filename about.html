About us

Keeping Africa's food heritage alive

“Our mission is to celebrate the recipes, ingredients and people behind African cuisine and to bring them to every kitchen in the world.”

A brief history

Thousands of years at the table

  1. 7000 BCE

    Sorghum, teff and yams are domesticated across the continent.

  2. 1000 CE

    Swahili coast trade brings cloves, cardamom and rice to East Africa.

  3. 1500s

    Cassava, maize and chili peppers arrive and transform African kitchens.

  4. Today

    African flavours shape menus from London to Rio — jollof, suya and berbere go global.
